Status: assets and copy ready; external accounts have not been posted to.

## Publishing rule

Share a resource because it answers a shopper's question, not to manufacture a
backlink. Use the canonical Wonder Aisle URL, disclose the affiliate nature of
the destination where context requires it, and follow each community's rules.
Do not reuse AliExpress seller imagery off-site unless its licence has been
confirmed. The six supplied creatives are original Wonder Aisle artwork.

## Community participation

Do not open with a link. First find a current question where the guide genuinely
solves the problem, answer it in full, disclose the connection, and link only if
the rules permit it.

Suggested response shape:

> The number can be misleading because sellers may mean height, length or the
> longest dimension, and the photograph is rarely to scale. Select the exact
> option first, find a labelled measurement image, and mark that length out with
> a tape measure where the plush will sit. I help run Wonder Aisle, and we made a
> visual 15–45 cm comparison for this exact question: [link]. It is an affiliate
> discovery site, although the guide itself does not require a purchase.

Small-creator outreach:

> Subject: A visual plush-size resource for your readers
>
> Hi [name] — I help run Wonder Aisle, a small affiliate discovery site. We made
> an original visual comparison of 15 cm, 20 cm, 25 cm and 45 cm plush sizes,
> plus a checklist for interpreting marketplace measurements. Your [specific
> post/video] addresses the same sizing problem, so the guide may be useful to
> your audience: [URL]. No payment or reciprocal link is requested. If it is not
> a fit, no reply is needed.

## First-month cadence

1. Publish the guide pins on different days with different copy.
2. Publish collection pins only after each collection is live and useful.
3. Make no more than five tailored creator contacts in the first month.
4. Participate in communities as a member; never repeat the same promotional
   reply across groups.
5. Record the destination URL, date, placement and referral traffic so the next
   month is guided by useful visits rather than raw post count.
